Depending on your consent and device settings, information may be used for personalised advertising or tracking across apps and websites owned by other companies. You can manage tracking permission in iOS Settings and advertising/privacy choices presented in the App.

Service providers

We may share information with providers that process it on our behalf or provide app functionality, including:

  • Google (AdMob) Privacy Policy

Information is retained only for as long as needed for the purposes described above, legal obligations, fraud prevention and dispute resolution. Service providers apply their own retention periods. We use reasonable safeguards, but no electronic system is completely secure.

Children

The App is not directed specifically to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can take appropriate action.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority. To exercise a right, contact us at the address below. We may need to verify your request.

International transfers

Our service providers may process information outside your country. Where required, appropriate legal safeguards are used for international transfers.

Changes

We may update this Policy when the App or legal requirements change. The updated date above will show when the latest version took effect.
